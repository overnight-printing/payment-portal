# Research Decisions

- **Decision:** Durable PostgreSQL row-lock claims and unique active attempts. **Rationale:** Browser/process locks cannot cover multiple Workers/tabs; financial unknowns must survive restarts. **Alternatives:** in-memory locks and unconditional PATCH are insufficient.
- **Decision:** Share Pages/Worker handlers. **Rationale:** The Worker remains a supported entrypoint and cannot be deleted without deployment evidence. **Alternatives:** patch duplicate handlers forever or remove an unknown consumer.
- **Decision:** Signed short-lived HttpOnly staff cookie plus durable login rate buckets. **Rationale:** Removes persistent reusable browser credentials without an unrequested identity platform; individual SSO remains a named release requirement.
- **Decision:** Bundle maintained PDF.js and matching worker, disable eval, bound file/page/pixel resources. **Rationale:** Runtime CDN 3.11.174 is affected by Mozilla GHSA-wgrm-67xf-hhpq; review imports/extraction after upgrade.
- **Decision:** Inspect the delivered official hosted iframe code and gateway references. **Rationale:** Current provider blur-tokenization does not support the parent's invented tokenize messages. Further exact protocol conclusions are recorded during implementation.
- **Decision:** Test SQL in an isolated PostgreSQL 17 cluster. **Rationale:** Available under /Applications/Postgres.app; no need to connect to the existing local server or production.
- **Decision:** No remote migration, live charge, email, push or deploy. **Rationale:** User explicitly requested a reviewable local result first.

## Verified provider boundary
CardSecure card tokens are 16 digits with a leading 9: https://support.cardpointe.com/cardsecure/ . The delivered hosted iframe tokenizes on blur and emits `message`/enhanced `token` fields; no parent tokenize command exists: https://fts.cardconnect.com/itoke/js/itoke.js . Authorization `name` is a separate string of at most 30 characters; no issuer name-match result is documented: https://developer.fiserv.com/product/CardPointe/api/post/cardconnect/rest/auth . Amount responses may be decimal dollars or integer minor units; order IDs can have at most 50 characters and should contain letters. This implementation uses 19 characters with a letter prefix. Approval may be partial: authorize without capture, validate full amount/reference, then explicitly capture and require Queued for Capture/Accepted. https://developer.fiserv.com/product/CardPointe/api/post/cardconnect/rest/capture . Uncertain responses require inquiry by order ID; this change deliberately keeps unresolved/noncaptured attempts locked for merchant review rather than auto-releasing them on a not-found/timeout result. https://developer.fiserv.com/product/CardPointe/docs/documentation/CardPointeGatewayDeveloperGuides.md#handling-cardpointe-gateway-timeouts .
